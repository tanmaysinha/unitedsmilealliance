Part-Time General Dentist

Secane Dental · Secane, PA 2 days/week · Flexible days $700/day guaranteed
Apply

Enjoy practicing dentistry without the pressure.

Secane Dental is a well-established neighborhood dental practice with over 60 years of history serving the local community. We're looking for a friendly, experienced general dentist to join our team two days per week.

This is an ideal opportunity for a dentist seeking a more relaxed, predictable schedule, whether you're transitioning away from full-time practice, looking for a better work-life balance, or simply want to focus on patient care without the administrative responsibilities of running an office.

What Makes This Opportunity Different?

We believe dentistry should be rewarding, not stressful. Our practice emphasizes long-term patient relationships, quality care, and a comfortable working environment.

You'll enjoy:

  • Guaranteed $700 daily compensation, with no production quotas or sales pressure.
  • Just 2 days per week, on the days that suit you, with a consistent, predictable schedule.
  • Office renovations currently underway, including refreshed interiors, updated flooring, ceiling improvements, and upgraded dental equipment to create a modern, comfortable clinical environment.
  • Established patient base with an emphasis on preventive and routine restorative dentistry.
  • Clinical independence to make treatment decisions in your patients' best interests.
  • No weekends or on-call responsibilities.
  • Relaxed, patient-focused atmosphere where quality matters more than volume.
  • Convenient location with easy parking.

What You'll Be Doing

Our practice primarily serves an established community of patients seeking ongoing preventive and general dental care. Responsibilities include:

  • Comprehensive dental examinations and routine checkups.
  • Preventive care, including routine dental cleanings.
  • General restorative dentistry, including fillings, crowns, and bridges.
  • Diagnosing dental conditions and developing appropriate treatment plans.
  • Building lasting relationships with patients through compassionate, personalized care.

We aren't looking for someone to maximize production or aggressively sell treatment. We're looking for a dentist who genuinely enjoys caring for patients.

Who We're Looking For

  • Active Pennsylvania dental license in good standing.
  • Experience in general dentistry, particularly preventive and restorative procedures.
  • Comfortable performing routine cleanings when needed.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and a patient-first approach.
  • Reliable, professional, and interested in a long-term part-time arrangement.

Experienced dentists looking to reduce their clinical workload are especially encouraged to apply.
