Part-Time Dental Hygienist

Paradise Dental · Philadelphia, PA 2 days/week · Flexible days $50/hour
Apply

We are seeking a dedicated and energetic Dental Hygienist to join our vibrant dental team! In this vital role, you will provide exceptional preventive dental care, educate patients on oral health, and support the overall well-being of our diverse patient community.

Your expertise will help ensure a comfortable, efficient, and positive experience for every patient while maintaining the highest standards of infection control and clinical excellence. This position offers an exciting opportunity to make a meaningful difference in patients' lives through compassionate care and professional expertise.

Responsibilities

  • Perform thorough dental cleanings, including scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar buildup.
  • Conduct comprehensive patient assessments, including vital signs and medical history reviews, to ensure safe treatment planning.
  • Assist the dentist during various procedures using four-handed dentistry techniques to optimize efficiency.
  • Take and develop dental radiographs while adhering to proper aseptic techniques and infection control protocols.
  • Educate patients on proper oral hygiene practices, preventive care, and treatment options tailored to pediatric dentistry when applicable.
  • Review medical records and documentation for accuracy, ensuring compliance with HIPAA regulations and proper medical coding standards.
  • Maintain accurate dental records within electronic record systems such as Eaglesoft or Dentrix.

Skills

  • Proficiency in dental terminology, anatomy, and general dentistry procedures.
  • Experience with dental office management software such as Eaglesoft or Dentrix is highly preferred.
  • Strong knowledge of infection control practices, aseptic technique, and sterilization procedures.
  • Ability to perform chairside assisting efficiently in pediatric dentistry settings when needed.
  • Excellent patient care skills with a compassionate approach to patient assessment and education.
  • Familiarity with medical terminology, medical records management, and medical coding processes.
  • Knowledge of HIPAA regulations to ensure patient privacy and confidentiality are maintained at all times.
